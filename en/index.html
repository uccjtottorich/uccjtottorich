Welcome

Welcome to Tottori Church

Tottori Church is a Protestant congregation of the United Church of Christ in Japan, located in Nishimachi, Tottori City. Since becoming a self-supporting church in 1890, we have cherished worship and prayer, the study of Scripture, and service to the local community.

Whether you are new to church or unfamiliar with the Bible, you are warmly welcome. No reservation is required, and you may attend worship in whatever clothes you normally feel comfortable wearing.

Worship

Worship and Gatherings

Sunday worship is open to everyone. Bibles and hymnals are available at the church.

Sunday Worship

Every Sunday, 10:30–11:30 a.m.

Through hymns, prayers, Scripture readings, and the sermon, we listen to God’s Word and worship together.

Church School (CS)

Every Sunday, 9:00–9:30 a.m.

Children learn about God’s love through songs and Bible stories and grow together in faith.

Bible Study and Prayer Meeting

Every Thursday, 1:30–3:00 p.m.

We read Scripture together, reflect on its message, and join our hearts in prayer for one another and for peace in the world.
